Other considerations and recommendation

Support the hybrid system through community-led delivery.

The design is recommended as a practical way to reduce ranger workload, strengthen visitor behaviour and support culturally respectful tourism management.

Recommendation

Proceed with the Hybrid Visitor Information System.

The community as a whole is likely to have a positive attitude towards this design, as the system can reduce the work pressure on forest rangers to repeatedly explain information, improve visitor behavior, and promote more sustainable tourism management.

At the same time, this plan aligns with YAC's long-term goal of "Return to Country", which helps enhance local visitor management capabilities and develop tourism in a more culturally respectful manner.

Community considerations

Consultation remains essential.

Some community members may still be concerned about over-tourism, inappropriate visitor behavior, or the misuse of cultural information. Therefore, continuous community communication and consultation remain very important during the implementation and later maintenance of the system.

Client and community actions

Further support is needed beyond the design scope.

To ensure the long-term success of the project, further actions are still needed from the client and the community. YAC and relevant stakeholders should continue to improve tourism infrastructure, such as road conditions, emergency communication systems, and tourist service facilities.

Strengthening support for the Ranger Program and Junior Ranger Program can also enhance the community's long-term management and maintenance capabilities.

Future research

Test the system in real-world conditions.

Additional research is needed. In the future, the system can be tested with a larger group of tourists in different seasons to evaluate its usability, maintainability and stability in real-world conditions.

Further research can also be conducted on multi-language information design, accessibility needs of elderly tourists, and lower-cost offline technical solutions to further optimize the system.

Long-term evaluation

Measure whether the system creates the intended change.

It is recommended to establish a long-term evaluation mechanism to continuously observe whether the system has truly reduced the work pressure of forest rangers and effectively improved the behavior of tourists.
